# MIM6 – Securing data

**Our claim: Svada-mini follows MIM6 (self-assessed), with the PoC limitations listed below. No requirement has the status "Not followed".**

How the statuses work is explained in [How Svada-mini follows the MIMs](README.md). The text of MIM6 is from MIMs v9.0 as we read it on 2026-10-28
(<https://mims.oascities.org/mims-specification-v9.0/securing-data>); see the version caveats in the overview. MIM6 is limited on purpose to secure data transfer and
has no conformance test.

## Capabilities and requirements

MIM6 has three capabilities. Requirements refer to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 controls.

### C1 – Data is only accessible to users that should have access to it

| Req. | What it says | Status | What we do, and what is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1.1 | Access control rules for physical and logical access are established and implemented (ISO 27001 5.15). | **Followed** (logical). **PoC limitation** (physical, written policy). | Logical access: each data distribution has an ODRL policy (distribution A: only the City, 30 days, no distribution or publication, approval by the data rights holder). The City's gateway checks the agreement at every use and refuses when the check fails. Distribution A is non-public and stays with the provider until the data rights holder approves, and the provider enforces that. Tests: `verify_ap4b.sh`, `verify_ap4c.sh`, `verify_ap10.sh`. Missing: there is no premises and no written access control policy of an organisation. |
| R1.2 | Allocation and management of authentication information is controlled by a management process (ISO 27001 5.17). | **PoC limitation** | Keys and passwords are generated by `tools/nya-hemligheter.sh` and kept in a local file that is not committed. There is no management process, no rotation and no instruction to staff, because there is no operating organisation. |
| R1.3 | Access rights are provisioned, reviewed, modified and removed (ISO 27001 5.18). | **Followed** (provision, change, removal). **PoC limitation** (review). | Provisioned by negotiation and approval; changed by a new agreement; removed by revoking the agreement (at the provider) or the participant's membership credential (status list). Tests: `verify_ap10.sh` (agreements), `verify_ap11.sh` (credentials). Missing: a periodic review of who holds access, which needs a real organisation. |

### C2 – Data accessed by users has not been altered

| Req. | What it says | Status | What we do, and what is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| R2.1 | Rules for the effective use of cryptography, including key management, are defined and implemented (ISO 27001 8.24). | **Followed** (use of cryptography). **PoC limitation** (key life cycle, internal transport). | Credentials and the revocation list are signed (JWT, did:web). The public demo uses HTTPS. Missing: traffic between containers on the internal network is plain HTTP, the connector's vault is in memory, and keys have no life cycle (generation, rotation, retirement). That is a deliberate simplification of this PoC (resource strategy: one machine, in-memory stores). |
| (C2) | The capability itself says the data has not been altered when it is accessed, not only while it is transferred. | **PoC limitation** | The dashboard's audit view has a hash chain that shows tampering while it runs (`verify_hash_kedja.sh`), but it does not survive a restart. Stored data (the context broker and the time series database) has no integrity protection beyond access to those components. A real operation would add signed or write-once storage and monitoring. |

### C3 – Data accessed by users originates from a verified source
C3 depends on R1.3 and R2.1 and adds one requirement.

| Req. | What it says | Status | What we do, and what is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| R3.1 | Secure authentication technologies and procedures are implemented based on information access restrictions (ISO 27001 8.5). | **Followed** | The Decentralized Claims Protocol (DCP): each connector presents verifiable credentials at every Dataspace Protocol call, and the other party checks signature, issuer and revocation. A role credential is only accepted from the right issuer (the City for the data rights holder's role, the provider for its own). Tests: `verify_ap8_fas3.sh`, `verify_ap11.sh`. PoC limitation: the credential issuer is simulated and not anchored in eIDAS, and the management APIs use shared keys. |

## Things we have not assessed
- **EU regulation named for MIM6 Plus** (NIS2, the Cyber Resilience Act, the Radio Equipment Directive). The MIM calls these pointers to relevant specifications, not specifications. We have not assessed them, and make no statement about them.
- **Identity life cycle.** MIMs v8 had a requirement that "the full life cycle of identities shall be managed". We did not find it in the v9.0 text we read, which may be a gap in our reading. We support issuing, revoking and reinstating membership credentials (`verify_ap11.sh`); expiry and rotation are PoC limitations.
- **The ISO/IEC 27001 and IEC 62443 standards themselves.** MIM6 refers to them. We have not assessed against them, and Svada-mini is not certified.

## Status "Not followed"
None. Every gap above exists only because Svada-mini is a PoC (no operating organisation, no production environment, in-memory stores, simulated issuer).

## Svenska (sammanfattning)
**Vårt påstående: Svada-mini följer MIM6 (egen bedömning), med PoC-begränsningarna ovan. Inget krav har statusen "Följs inte".** C1 (åtkomst) och C3
(verifierad källa) täcks av ODRL-policyer, avtalskontroll vid varje användning, hävning, och DCP med verifierbara intyg. Det som saknas beror på att det här är en
PoC: ingen förvaltande organisation (hanteringsprocess för nycklar, periodisk granskning av åtkomst), nyckelns livscykel, krypterad intern trafik, en vault som
bara finns i minnet och integritetsskydd av lagrade data. Vi har inte bedömt NIS2/CRA eller själva ISO 27001/IEC 62443, och vi är inte certifierade. MIM:arna har
inget konformitetsprov.
